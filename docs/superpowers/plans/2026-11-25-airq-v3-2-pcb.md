# airQ v3 Plan 2: PCB implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two KiCad 10 boards, fab-ready for JLCPCB Economic-tier assembly:
- the **main board**, shared by airQ and airQ mini
- the **sense tongue** (SHT45 + VEML7700 on a Qwiic lead)

Both must be DRC-clean, have every placed part Economic-eligible, and export STEP models for the enclosure.

**Architecture:**
- Python checkers in `tools/hw/` turn the spec's hard rules into tests. They read KiCad's own exports (netlist, `.kicad_pcb`, JLC BOM CSV) and compare them with `hardware/interface.yaml` and a curated `hardware/bom/parts.csv`. They are written first (TDD), so every later task has a red/green gate.
- Board design follows whichever authoring route the Task 2 spike proves.
- Routing uses Freerouting. A JDK is already installed on this machine.

**Tech stack:**
- KiCad 10.0.x with `kicad-cli`
- easyeda2kicad (installed with `uv tool install easyeda2kicad`)
- Freerouting
- Fabrication Toolkit or Bouni's kicad-jlcpcb-tools
- Python with pytest (stdlib only for the checkers)

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-25-airq-v3-design.md` (§6.1–6.3, §6.7, §6.8). Deviations, notably the sense tongue: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-airq-v3-overview.md`.

**Prerequisites:**
- Plan 1 Task 1 is done, so `hardware/interface.yaml` exists.
- The owner has installed KiCad 10.

## Global constraints

- **Boards:** 2-layer, 1.6 mm FR-4, 1 oz copper, lead-free HASL. **All SMT parts on `F.Cu`**; nothing on `B.Cu`.
- **JLCPCB Economic tier:** one side only, 2–50 boards per order, no V-cut, and **every placed part Economic-eligible**. No more than **15 unique Extended parts per board** (the fee budget).
- **Outlines:** main board ≤ **60.0 × 30.0 mm**; tongue ≤ **22.0 × 14.0 mm**. Corners may be notched; the board may not grow.
- **Power:**
  - USB-C 5 V sink, with a **separate 5.1 kΩ to GND on CC1 and on CC2**.
  - USB ESD on D±/VBUS.
  - A **synchronous buck, 5 V → 3.3 V, rated at least 1 A** (no LDO on the main rail).
  - At least 22 µF at the buck output, and at least 10 µF on each of the SEN66 connector's VDD and the module's 3V3.
- **MCU:** ESP32-C6-MINI-1-N4 (LCSC C5736265). Use Espressif's antenna keep-out from the module datasheet, with the antenna at a board edge (overhang preferred).
- **Flashing:** normally over native USB (GPIO12/13).
- **Programming/debug header (spec §6.8):** `J_AUX_UART`, unpopulated 2.54 mm pads carrying `+3V3`, `GND`, `AUX_UART_TX` (GPIO16 = U0TXD), `AUX_UART_RX` (GPIO17 = U0RXD), `EN` and `BUTTON` (GPIO9/BOOT). It gives ROM-UART recovery, and the same pads take a future mmWave presence sensor.
- **I²C:** 4.7 kΩ pull-ups to +3V3 on `I2C_SDA` and `I2C_SCL`, on the main board only.
- **Sense tongue:**
  - SHT45-AD1B-R2 (C5221601) behind a milled slot on 3 sides, with copper cleared at least 1.5 mm around it.
  - VEML7700-TR (C504893) at the opposite end, facing the same way as the board's front edge.
  - 100 nF on each part.
- **LEDs:** 5 V addressable RGB LEDs on the main board's front edge, fed through a 74AHCT1G125-class buffer from `LED_DATA`. Plus `J_LED_EXT` (JST SH 3-pin: +5V, LED_DOUT_5V, GND) for an off-board LED segment.
- **Button:** a side-actuated tactile switch on `BUTTON` (GPIO9) to GND, with a 10 kΩ pull-up.
- **E-ink:** a 24-pin 0.5 mm FPC (`J_EPD`) plus GoodDisplay's reference driver circuit (DESPI-C02 schematic), copied exactly, with BS1 tied low (4-wire SPI). It is populated on every board.
- **Pins, addresses and connector pinouts come only from `hardware/interface.yaml`.** Each connector symbol and the MCU carry a `Role` field matching their interface key (`MCU`, `J_SEN66`, …).
- **Every placed part has an `LCSC` field.** Unpopulated parts (`J_AUX_UART`) are marked DNP.

## Review focus

1. **A part going out of stock or losing Economic eligibility between design and order:** `check_bom` refuses a parts table checked more than 7 days before the order. Tested in Tasks 1 and 7.
2. **USB-C to USB-C cables and USB-PD chargers:** they supply 5 V only when each CC pin has its own 5.1 kΩ pull-down. Tested in Task 1 (rule test) and Task 4 (netlist).
3. **SEN66 fan pulses (~350 mA) coinciding with Wi-Fi transmit:** these could brown out the board without bulk capacitance on both loads. Tested in Task 1 (rule test) and Task 4.
4. **Boot straps:** GPIO9 must read high at boot unless the button is held; GPIO8 and GPIO15 must not be driven. Tested in Task 4 (netlist rule).
5. **Mirrored FPC or connector pinouts** (the FPC's top versus bottom contact; cable pin 1): every connector's pin→net order is checked against `interface.yaml`, and the FPC contact side is checked against the panel datasheet. Tested in Task 4; the contact side is checked at the owner review in Task 5.

### Task 1: Hardware checkers (TDD)

**Files:**
- Create:
  - `tools/hw/sexpr.py`: a stdlib-only S-expression reader for KiCad files
  - `tools/hw/check_netlist.py`
  - `tools/hw/check_bom.py`
  - `tools/hw/check_board.py`
- Test: `tests/hardware/test_sexpr.py`, `test_check_netlist.py`, `test_check_bom.py`, `test_check_board.py`, plus small hand-written fixtures in `tests/hardware/fixtures/` (a 3-component netlist, a 2-footprint `.kicad_pcb`, a BOM CSV, a parts CSV)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `parse(text: str) -> list` (nested lists of str atoms)
  - `parse_value(s: str) -> float`: `"5.1k"` → 5100.0, `"10u"` → 1e-5, `"100n"` → 1e-7, `"4k7"` → 4700.0
  - `check_netlist(netlist: Path, interface: Path) -> list[str]`: error strings, empty when clean. Rules:
    1. Every `signals` entry: the MCU pin `IO<n>` sits on the net with that name.
    2. Every `connectors` entry: the part whose `Role` matches has pin i on net i (`NC` means unconnected).
    3. `USB_CC1` and `USB_CC2` each reach GND through their own resistor of 5.1 kΩ ± 1 %.
    4. `I2C_SDA` and `I2C_SCL` each have a 4.7 kΩ resistor to `+3V3`.
    5. The `+3V3` net carries at least 2 capacitors of 10 µF or more, plus 22 µF or more in total.
    6. The `BUTTON` net has a pull-up to `+3V3`. GPIO8 and GPIO15 are unconnected or carry only a pull resistor.
  - `check_parts(parts_csv: Path, max_extended: int = 15, fresh_days: int | None = None, today: date | None = None) -> list[str]`. The parts CSV columns are `lcsc,mpn,library,economic,checked_on,stock,unit_usd`.
  - `check_bom(bom_csv: Path, parts_csv: Path, **kw) -> list[str]`: every BOM row has an LCSC number that appears in the parts table, plus all `check_parts` rules.
  - `check_board(pcb: Path, max_w: float, max_h: float) -> list[str]`: the Edge.Cuts bounding box fits within the limits, and every footprint is on `F.Cu`.
  - Each `check_*.py` is also a CLI. It prints the errors and exits 1 if there are any. The CLI forms are:
    - `check_netlist.py NETLIST INTERFACE`
    - `check_bom.py BOM PARTS [--fresh-days N]`
    - `check_bom.py --parts-only PARTS [--fresh-days N]`
    - `check_board.py PCB --max-w W --max-h H`

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests**, one per rule, each with a fixture that violates exactly that rule:
  ```python
  def test_parse_value(): assert parse_value("5.1k") == 5100 and parse_value("4k7") == 4700 and parse_value("10u") == 1e-5
  def test_netlist_clean_fixture_passes(): assert check_netlist(FX/"good.net", FX/"iface.yaml") == []
  def test_wrong_gpio_net_reported(): assert any("IO6" in e for e in check_netlist(FX/"swapped_i2c.net", FX/"iface.yaml"))
  def test_shared_cc_resistor_reported(): assert any("CC2" in e for e in check_netlist(FX/"shared_cc.net", FX/"iface.yaml"))
  def test_missing_bulk_cap_reported(): assert any("+3V3" in e for e in check_netlist(FX/"no_bulk.net", FX/"iface.yaml"))
  def test_connector_pin_order(): assert any("J_SEN66" in e for e in check_netlist(FX/"mirrored_sen66.net", FX/"iface.yaml"))
  def test_parts_not_economic(): assert any("C999" in e for e in check_parts(FX/"parts_standard_only.csv"))
  def test_parts_stale(): assert check_parts(FX/"parts_ok.csv", fresh_days=7, today=date(2027,1,1)) != []
  def test_too_many_extended(): assert check_parts(FX/"parts_16_ext.csv") != []
  def test_bom_missing_lcsc(): assert check_bom(FX/"bom_missing.csv", FX/"parts_ok.csv") != []
  def test_board_too_big(): assert check_board(FX/"board_61x30.kicad_pcb", 60, 30) != []
  def test_part_on_bottom(): assert any("B.Cu" in e for e in check_board(FX/"board_bottom_part.kicad_pcb", 60, 30))
  ```
- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `uv run --with pytest --with pyyaml pytest tests/hardware -v`. Expected: **all FAIL** (import errors).
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `sexpr.py`, then the three checkers. Netlist fixtures use the format `kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadsexpr` produces; base them on KiCad 10's documented structure.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run** the tests again. Expected: **all pass**.
- [ ] **Step 5: Commit** with the message `feat(hw): netlist/BOM/board checkers with fixtures`.
